After files are copied, Setup will automatically install Python, packages, and
models (internet required, usually 5–20 minutes). Please wait — do not close
the progress window until it finishes.

On the final page you can optionally check "Launch HerbivoR".

On PCs without an NVIDIA GPU, CPU mode is selected automatically.
